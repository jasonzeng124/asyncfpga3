  ANCHOR  B's literal fix: if H->U' fails, rebuild the U' cover claims of comb D inputs from the D input's own last
          change (claim(u, sb_u, same end)).  The rebuilt claim is kept only if the step passes and no new obligation fails.

          opt-in variant: tested on X25Y76.B5 only, and it was rejected.
  COMBREL/COMBTRUNC  a comb-chain segment join (ChainOK) that fails is first retried with the relative Up (never
          passed).  If it still fails, the chain is TRUNCATED to its passing prefix (a ChainOK chain of that prefix), the
          same treatment latch chains get.  Before this, the claim was marked failing.
  ARTFIX/ARTFIX2 (r1b switches, set in the "art" and "fin" runs): a claim end prefers events causally after the guard's
          E, or after the next latch phase's start.
  sneed/SEPNEED: every Sep used by an accepted claim obligation (CovTime start and end, latch ChainOK, comb ChainOK, loop
          covers), with the Sep value it needs and the depth.
