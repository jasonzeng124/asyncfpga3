### Rows and hypotheses

Rows are boxes `(lo, hi) : X × X`, 180 in total:
- `<seed>_<inst>_{FMIN,FMAX,SMIN,SMAX}`: `lo = ⌊v_C⌋`, `hi = ⌈v_C⌉` (H_ONE_CORNER, integer
  rounding hull);
- `<seed>_<inst>_BOX`: `[⌊FMIN⌋, ⌈SMAX⌉]` per element (H_INDEP).

The named hypotheses:
- 7g's `Run` (H_A0_FALL, H_REQ_STEADY, the per-pin transport LUTs with the real INITs, H_LOOP_*);
- `fdl1`/`fdl2` (H_INST_FORK_k: `c` is the pair's shared A0 prefix, and every other field is the
  routed suffix, site pin or arc);
- H_ARC_C (7g's one ua_k arc, common to its A0 and grant pins);
- 7h/7j's `Run`, `Scn`/`Scn2`, `Server` (H_SERVER_MIN), and `Real7` (H_INST_RT2: rise = fall,
  the flat O6 arcs, any r prefix);
- H_SERVER_GRAPH (`srv ≤ smin`; untrusted evidence, a hypothesis of the theorems, not proved);
- the UNTRUSTED extraction.

### Theorems (plain language)

- **`fork1_box_iff` / `fork2_box_iff`** (exact, both directions, any box with `lo ≤ hi` and
  positive loops). Every record in the box and every 7g run has client k's ack never rising iff
  `fm_k = early_k(lo) − late_k(hi) ≥ 0`.
  - The → direction builds a run on the worst record (`run_exists`).
  - **`fork1_counter` / `fork2_counter`**: if `fm_k < 0`, the worst box record has `A_k` high,
    on every run, at `T0 + aC + c + early`.
  - Per failing row: `<row>_fork<k>_neg : fm_k < 0` (15 rows, all `_BOX`).
- **`rt2_iff` / `rt2b_iff`** (exact). On a realising record, RT2 holds iff its four margins
  `mP mG mA mGG` (server-free) plus `smin` / `2·smin` are ≥ 0.
  - `a0fork_iff`: 7h's A0Fork is `mF ≥ 0`.
  - `mF_fork`: `mF` is 7g's client-2 margin (`nF`: client 1).
- **`rt2_box_iff` / `rt2b_box_iff`** (exact for the arc-form condition). For a server bound S,
  RT2 holds for every realising record of every box point with `smin = S` iff each margin at its
  own worst point (`wP wG wA wGG`), plus `k·S`, is ≥ 0. The witnesses are `mk7` records.
  - Per row: `<row>_rt2 : BoxRT2 lo hi need` and `<row>_rt2_tight : ¬ BoxRT2 lo hi (need−1)`,
    likewise for `rt2b`. So `need` is exact.
- **`rt2_excl` / `rt2b_excl` / `box_excl_srv` / `box_excl2_srv`**: A1 and A2 are never high
  together across a client-1 (client-2) release, for every server with `smin ≥ S`. These
  instantiate 7h `rt2_exclusion_arcs` and 7j `rt2_exclusion2_arcs`.
- **`row_srv`**, with `Row.okSrv` and `<row>_okSrv` (decided per row).
- **`gcd_corner_srv`** (the headline). For all 144 corner rows: both forks are safe on every
  record of the hull and every run, and A1/A2 are exclusive in both handovers for every server
  with `smin ≥ srv`.
  - `cornerRows_allOk_count = 141`: server-free.
  - `boxRows_okSrv_count = 20`.
- **Non-vacuity:**
  - the fork side is built in: `run_exists` is used inside `fork_k_box_iff`, and every row has
    `wf` (decided);
  - `Real7` is satisfiable on every box point (`mk7_real`);
  - the 7h/7j model's own Run + Scn witnesses are `ArbCorners.nonvacuous_corners`;
  - a per-row trace witness for RT2 was not built.

## Findings

1. **For shard C (tools), a bug in `tcheck/extract.Prjx.split`.** When a driver leaves its site
   through TWO site pins (seen: O6 on both B and BMUX, 16 split pairs over 4 seeds x 9 arbiters: 12 A0 pairs, 4 r_k nets), the two
   sinks are on different RC nets. `split` then still counts sink a's driver-side OUTMUX hop
   (73-86 ps FMIN, up to 241 ps SMAX) as "shared" and drops sink b's hop.
   - **Jx (rule J')**: C's margin is pessimistic by about 84 ps where ustate is sink a, and
     **optimistic** where ua is sink a: seed 3 merge11 c2 (C 421 vs 336 fast), seed 4
     merge12.uarb2 c1 and c2 (353/235 vs 270/152). It stays positive in-column.
   - **grantfork / runtack r_k rows** (drg/drs): seed 2 merge10.uarb1 c1 `drg` is
     understated by 75 ps (FMIN) / 211 ps (SMAX). So **Bag's RT1 m1 there is optimistic**: true
     FMIN about +174, not +249, and still positive. Box need is about 594, not 383.
   - The other three affected r rows (d merge10 c2, 2 merge2 c2, 4 merge10 c2) are pessimistic.
   - The fix is `Bar/a0extract.py:split`.
2. **Apart from finding 1, C's Jx is about 10 ps (fast) / 23 ps (slow) more pessimistic than
   7g.** C treats ua_k's arcs from its A0 and grant pins as independent intervals; 7g's
   H_ARC_C makes them one arc (the arcs are equal per prjxray, and the extractor checks this).
   If they were independent within the full box, the fork box margins would drop by a further
   79 ps: 22/72 negative, worst −580.
3. **Seed 2 merge10.uarb1 RT2 fails server-free at a consistent corner** (g1 term: g1's fall
   must close ua1's loop before A0 re-rises). The cause is the r1 net routed to ugrant through
   the other site pin (388 ps FMIN). It needs smin ≥ 19 / 57 / 115 ps (FMIN / SMAX / SMIN),
   against a graph `srv` of 2311 ps or more.
4. **Box fork failures: 15/72** clients. Worst seed 4 merge12.uarb2 (−337 / −501 ps); seed 2
   merge12.uarb1 is −237 / −189. These depend on the corner model, exactly as 7g's
   `client_k_box` says.
