prjxray bands (guard g, scatter κ), every arbiter resolution within T, and every environment that follows the
four-phase protocol, including one that offers the next pair as soon as the previous input handshake completes.

## The two findings
1. **The target cannot carry the statement as worded.** Its three input requests are one net (gcd_rig's bd_fork);
   its operand buses come from the rig's 16-entry table with merged and tied bits. Spec.lean is written for what
   the bitstream is: env nodes as wired, pairs restricted to what the wiring can represent.
2. **Under a pipelined environment the target is wrong.** On the decoded netlist (every arc inside its band):
   vector 9 then vector 4 gives out0 = [1, 4096]; vector 9 twice and vector 15 then 5 deadlock; (2^19,2^19),(3,5)
   reorders at the fast end, the slow end and with ±20% scatter. The mechanism (C's analysis): a pair that skips
   the k-loop overtakes one that iterates in it; `start` is acknowledged when the control token is first stored,
   long before the result (141 ns vs 934 ns), contrary to gcd_rig.v's header. Also: data must be held until the
   acknowledge falls (broad four-phase); early release livelocks the kernel.

## The Lean refutation (design)
A witness behaviour is a finite description per node (value at 0, sorted flip times) plus one exact period P for
the free-running housekeeping ring (the only oscillation; 122 nodes, P = 22122 ps). `chkFrom` checks each gate's
equation at the breakpoints between flips, `chkFrom_sound` + `eq_ext` + `gateOK_slow` turn that into
`Spec.GateOK` at the slow corner with g = 1, κ = 0 (every pin delay = the slow end of its band: the strictest
H_DELAY). Boundary facts (Env, ArbOK, ¬Correct) reduce to finite checks over the boundary flips
(`flip_iff`, `rises_eq`, `walk_sound`, `envIn_of`, `envOut_of`, `arbOK_one`). The target-specific assembly is
`Wit/V9V4/Refute.lean`: `refuted : ¬ NetCorrect net boundary arbs pSlow 656 20000 0 2999`.
Kernel cost: per-gate cost is quadratic in its breakpoints; 122 gates near the ring and a clock BUFG dominate
(~30 s and ~3 GB each); the checks run in 1695 slices (one declaration each, memory bounded).

## What it does NOT show
- That `net` is the target's configuration: the decoder is untrusted; the round trip is in Python only.
- That the simulator or the witness reflects silicon: they are behaviours of the Spec's model (H_PRIM, H_DELAY).
- Anything about the sequential statement `NetCorrectSeq` (the one left to prove).
- Research's fid GLS has not been run with a pipelined environment (asked in FOR-RESEARCH 2).
