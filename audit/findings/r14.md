## Method / files
- tb_race.v (+ race.log, race_patched.log, trace.log): K=400 copies of FID_LUT6 (fid_core.vh) with a C(r1,~r2) INIT and a transport feedback wire. TLOOP = FB + pin arc, as in gen.py.
- ring_rc.py / ring_tau.py (+ logs): continuous delay + RC + comparator loop, written independently of Fid3's orb_tau.py.
- tb_seqx.v (+ seqx.log), tb_c4lat.v (+ c4lat.log), tb_ffx.v (+ ffx.log): prims.v, default and -DGLS_FID.
- .vvp files deleted; rebuild: `iverilog -gspecify [-DGLS_FID] -I$G -o x.vvp tb_*.v $G/prims.v` (tb_race: `iverilog -g2012 tb_race.v $G/fid_core.vh`), G=~/wt-r13/cells/gls.
