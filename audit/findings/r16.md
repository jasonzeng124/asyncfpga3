## Method

All benches mine, iverilog 12.0 (`source ~/audit-tc/openxc7/export.sh`), unmodified fid_core.vh copied from
~/wt-r15 (21cae6b), `timescale 1ps/1ps` before the include (as prims.v:30). Rerun: `sh audit/r16/run.sh`.

* tb_c2r.v: 40 independent copies (SEED = 1..40) of FID_LUT6 INIT 0x00E8, A1=a=1, A2=b, A3=q through a
  100 ps transport wire with a 100 ps arc (TLOOP6 = 200), other arcs 50; b pulse W at 7000 (mux/sampler
  arrival t0 = 7050). chk.py takes the decision each copy printed (+META_VERBOSE=2) and checks the full
  end-of-step edge list of q against: reject/back -> [t0 up, t0+w down]; commit trp>0 -> [t0 up, t0+w down,
  t0+w+trp up]; commit trp=0 -> [t0 up]; w >= TLOOP -> [t0 up]. Result per width in sweep.log: 0 mismatches
  in 360 pulses (outcomes e.g. W=20 35 reject/5 back; W=150 1 reject/12 back/24 commit/3 commit0).
  The final value depends on the seed for every runt width 60..199 (seed dependence also visible).
* Follow-pulse runs: +W=20 +GAP=1 +W2=150/200/600, and the +META_OFF=1 control (follow.log).
* gen_fidrand.py / cmp_fidrand.py: Lean formula O(t) = F(k -> A_k(t - D_k)) with A_k(s<0) = A_k(0),
  compared at every ps t >= max D_k (the Lean model is not faithful before, as stated in GLSFid).
