## 3. Rule F hop-term gap on #3's route
Script: `audit/bq23/rulef_drain.py`. Output: `rulef_drain3.out`.
- **Structure, from routed.json and not from tighten.** The script enumerates every bd_delay chain. A line counts
  as fast-fall if every stage g[i>=1] is a 2-input LUT with INIT 1000 (AND). For each stage it takes the ripple
  pin (the net of g[i-1].O6) and the tap pin (the other input). All taps must share one net. The tap source pin
  is the SDF driver of the tap pins.
  - The chain survey is in `survey_chains.py`. #2 has 29 FASTFALL lines (the bd_delay_gated compute `udly`).
    #3 has 65: those 29, plus 21 bd_mux `udly` and 15 RFAST `rdly`.
- **Delays, from my own SDF parse.** All triples are min=typ=max. Time 0 is the tap source falling.
  - Head: f0 = longest path from the source to the head input, plus the head arc.
  - Each later stage: f_i = min(tap_i + arc, f_{i-1} + hop_i + arc).
  - Rule F's drain is max f_i.
  - Corrected drain = max(max f_i, max_i (f_{i-1} + hop_i - tap_i + 1)). This means every ripple input must
    already be low when the re-rise reaches that stage's tap.
- **Re-rise.** t_reuse is rule F's own value (t_exit + t_back, taken from `tighten.audit_fastfall` on the same
  pruned graph). The guard is req_guard = max(0.2 x drain, 200).
- **Results:**
  - My uncorrected flush equals tighten's t_flush on all 65 lines (65/65 lines matched, none missing).
  - 484 stages were checked. Only 6 have hop > tap (the largest is hop - tap = +197 ps, ucontrol_merge4 g[6]),
    and none of those sits late in the fall.
  - The corrected drain exceeds rule F's flush on only 2 lines: umux0.udly (648 vs 567, margin 2856 -> 2775) and
    umux6.udly (420 vs 419).
  - The worst corrected margin is still ucontrol_merge12.udly at **+888 guarded / +1113 raw**. No line is
    negative.
- **Teeth (negative control).** I copied the SDF to the scratchpad (not into audit/) and set
  ucontrol_merge12 g[0] -> g[1] hop to 3000 ps (`rulef_drain3_neghop.out`).
  - Rule F's numbers do not move (flush 1127, guarded +888): that is the gap.
  - My corrected margin goes to raw -1453, guarded -2191, and the line is flagged.
- **Verdict: BQ#3 is safe against the omitted term on this route, in the nominal SDF model.** The chain stages are
  RLOC-adjacent, so hops (295-631 ps) are mostly shorter than the taps (295-1980 ps).
- **Caveats:**
  - The SDF lacks site-pin delays (rules.md 1a). Those can lengthen a hop relative to a tap by a few hundred ps,
    but that is well under the >= 888 ps headroom.
  - t_reuse uses rule F's own optimistic re-rise model (shortest tail->src path). I did not recompute it.
  - Other seeds were not checked.
- tighten.py as a whole exits 1 on this route, but only because of rule D's 38 boundaries (the documented screen). Rule F itself has 0 violations.
