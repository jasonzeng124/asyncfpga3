### (b) What is stated
- `SatPinW d pl x` (Events6Gate.lean:56-62) is a structure: `f : Nat → Nat → Nat → Nat` (gate gi reads input i at `f gi i t`), `warp` (each `f gi i` is a `Warp` of the gate's band) and `eval` (gate output = INIT at the pin values read through `f`). `toSat` forgets `f`; `ofSat` recovers one by `Classical.choose`. `wf hsat gi i := hsat.f gi i` (l.88), so `tau hsat p` and every trace hypothesis on it (ArbOrd, ArbEdge, QQuiet) are about the supplied `f`.
- `ArbOrd hsat a T0` (Events9ArbB1.lean:212-217): four inequalities on composed warps for all t > T0 (g1, g2, and the two R0 terms).
- `arbOrd_of_transport` (Events9ArbB1.lean:688-711): if `wf hsat gi i t = t − D gi i` for all gi, i, t, then the four rows `pathD D (P1g1) + 2 ≤ pathD D (P1Q) + pathD D (Pqg1)`, likewise g2 and the two R0 rows, plus `hT` (T0 ≥ sum of nine path delays + 4) give ArbOrd.
- `arb_low_of_reqlow` (l.720-764): ArbOK + both request wires low on [0, T0+2] ⇒ g1, g2, R0 low on [0, T0+2].
- Discrete `Warp lo hi f` (Core/Trace/Discrete.lean:98-101): monotone, `f t ≤ t − lo`, `t − hi ≤ f t`. Gate bands are per gate, `plG g _ = (g.lo, g.hi)`; `plo`/`phi` sum them along a path.
