## Finding
- `cells/hw/gen_bench.py` DOMAIN_RESTRICTIONS: `"xorshift": {"rounds": {"kind": "and_mask", "value": "32'h00000FFF"}}`.
- Research's corrected kernel_oracle.sh (d03e02f47) applies "xorshift rounds & 0xFFF" too.
- So on this bench, and on the board run through it, rounds <= 4095. The loop counter never exceeds 4095.
- The incrementer's longest reachable carry is i = 2^k - 1 for k <= 11, i.e. a 12-bit ripple.
- A vector with rounds = 4095 (about 4095 iterations, about 70 µs simulated at ~17 ns/iter) exercises every carry the board can produce.
- **So UADDI0 and the compares can be judged by vectors and draws on this bench, without a preload.**
  - The 31-bit carry is unreachable on the board, just as it is in simulation.
  - Keeping UADDI0 at its static full-range size (12) protects against inputs the bench cannot deliver.
- **Caveat:** this holds for the bench (FIXED mode, gen_bench masks). If the board is ever to take unmasked rounds, the full-range question returns. That decision belongs to the owner.
- Not checked: the masks in the board's own vector logs (August). Research's README says the board vectors are FIXED-mode vectors through this bench.
