## Notes

- **gcd_hw_conditional.** Read with its module doc, it is honest: it says "CONDITIONAL on the open premise".
  But its name, and the commit headline listing it among "capstone pieces", invite reading it as a gcd_hw
  result. It is a generic corollary of `net_refines`. The gcd_hw-specific facts proved in this range are
  token-level (`gcd_hw_graph_computes_gcd`, liveness), structural (`gcdhw_ring_ge3`) and per-cell (`link_op`,
  `muxn_op`, `amerge3_cascade`). Nothing yet connects them to a circuit-level statement.
- **ArbOverlapRace.** The cell result is solid within its model: transport delays, rise=fall, and O5/O6 reading
  one pin buffer (H_SHARED_Q_PIN, which is physically right for LUT6_2). The board's 15/192 is not explained by
  it. It is made *possible* by the rig witness, and only under a favourable pin choice.
- **RtFb driver hop.** Regenerate RtFbGcdS* with the 10h driver-side hop. Until then, treat "0 failures in every
  one-corner model" as unproven for the `fast` (RC lower bound 0) variant on seed 4.
- **Did not do:** a joint non-vacuity witness for `gcd_hw_conditional`'s premises. Only a degenerate one (all
  channels silent) is evident, because the Ops `Net` for gcd_hw was never built. I also did not mutate the
  2.5-minute ring certificate; the independent Verilog check replaces it.
