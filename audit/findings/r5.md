## Summary
- **xorshift (6119060):** 105.37 is confirmed, and seed 1 reproduces at 105.2. The ratios are right for the stated means, and the vectors and seeds really are the same in all three arms. The sized arms' seeds 5-8 are still unpublished, so 1.64x/1.76x rest on two means that cannot be recomputed. On published seeds 1-4 the ratios are 1.61x/1.77x. T3 and T3+FF seed 1 re-routed at current tools give the same values (65.9, 59.6), and T3+FF seed 1 is rule-F clean.
- **collatz64 (9452a14):** the 102.65 mean is confirmed. The subject's "skip 6/22" understates the gap: rule I leaves 22 of 34 stages unaudited, and rule H 6 of 35. Rule H's skip is a timing-graph reachability gap on 64-bit lanes, so a short ack could hide there. Only nominal routed GLS covers it.
- **GLS fid (5567170):** it really is transport (my two-pulse tb passes both pulses; the default drops the second). Corners are handled correctly. The default build is byte-identical on my own route. OUTMUX is still undercharged with nextpnr's SDF (flat 25 ps vs 69-209 ps) and is 0 for O5 even with the xray SDF. f3_model.md lists files changed that this commit does not contain.
