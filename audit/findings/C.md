## Axioms (`#print axioms` from the audit files)

Every headline theorem uses at most `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. This covers `ring_three`, `k_tokens`, `dead_of_le_two`, `D_reach`, `fair_window`, `kState_live_iff`, `muller_pipeline`, `muller_live`, `pipeline_fifo`, `Witness.witness`, `changes_in_window`, `check_sound`, `check_stable`, `win_exact`, `check_exact_disjoint`, `corr_fixed_safe`, `corr_bd_violation`, `toyLink_bundled` and `toyShort_broken`. No `sorryAx` and no `ofReduceBool` appear. A textual grep of `Formal/Net/*.lean` and `Formal/Timing/*.lean` finds no sorry, native_decide, admit, axiom, implemented_by or extern.

Caveat: the Timing theorems were checked from a verbatim concatenated copy (`AuditTiming.lean`, built by `gen/mk_timing.sh` from the commit's files), not from lake-built oleans.

## Other notes
- Hypotheses stated as named and unproved are not refuted, and the journal admits them: H_SI (interleaving), H_RESET_CLEAN, H_ONE_TOKEN (the compiler's rings hold one token), H_NODE (isochronic fork), H_LATCH, H_CEL_FAIR, and the single launch in the timing semantics.
- The `MullerStageW` variant (1-tick wire delay) gives only protocol safety (`mullerW_pipeline_protocol`), not FIFO or liveness.
- `CheckerToy` numbers are explicitly "ILLUSTRATIVE, not prjxray data". No real netlist has been checked.
