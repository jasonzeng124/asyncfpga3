### (b) What is proved, in plain words (Events9FullArb.lean:186-310, Events9ArbB1.lean)

All three verdicts end the same way as `design_verdict9arbnet_edge` (P14a §1). For the parsed routed netlist
`d` and every behaviour `x` with the premises below, there are virtual core signals and a `Beh7` realisation
in which, under SelPart, SelOK and the residual obligations of uncertified items, every real LUT wire equals its
reset value flipped `M t j` times. They differ only in the arbiter premises.

`design_verdict9arbnet_ord` (proof: `design_verdict9arbnet ... (arbCond_of_ord ...)`):
- (i) checked from data (decidable, evaluated by the xevents9par/xarbnet executables on files outside the repo): `Recog6`, `net.OK7`, `runLogP = some J`, the certificate items, `ArbOK` per arbiter (the real INITs and read paths), `OwnOK`, `RsOK`, `FixShape`, and `ArbRowsOKS` per arbiter (start bounds, `2 ≤ lo` side conditions, ustate reset released by T0; the four B1 rows are dropped).
- (ii) assumed: `SatPin d plG x`, wires 0/1 constant, design reset `x rw t = decide (t < T)`, `ArbEdge hsat rn (as k) T0 (H k)` (QClean, quiet start `low`, settle bound `stall`/`hi`, four `RdEdge` windows), and **`ArbOrd hsat (as k) T0`**.

`ArbOrd` (Events9ArbB1.lean:213-218): four inequalities, for every `t > T0`,
`tau hsat (P1Q a) (f1Of hsat a 2 t) ≤ f1Of hsat a 0 (t - 2)` (g1), the same for g2 with P2Q, and two shifted ones
for the R0 readers (o3/o4). In words: the time at which ustate reads the request, taken at the moment the grant
reads q, is no later than the grant's own request read 2 steps (2 ps) earlier. It names only warps and never a
signal value. The warps are `tau hsat p = wf hsat gi i ∘ ...`, with `wf = Classical.choose (hsat g ...)`
(Events6Gate.lean:54-55), i.e. an unspecified witness of each gate's `PinRel` existential.

`design_verdict9arbnet_ordq`: as `_ord`, but in place of ArbEdge it takes `hlo : 1 ≤ plo d (PqQ)` (checked,
part of xarbnet's ArbRowsOKS count), **`QQuiet hsat rw rn (as k)`** (assumed, at every time) and
`he : QClean ... → ArbEdge ...` (assumed: ArbEdge's other fields, given QClean). `qclean_of_arb` turns QQuiet
into QClean through the real ustate equation `arb_q_eq` (gate_spec + rdP_val at the ArbOK read paths) and the
generic `qclean_of_quiet`. QQuiet: after each change of q at τ ≥ 1, ustate's four other reads (r1, r2, rst, A0,
each through its own `tau hsat` path) at every u ∈ [τ, τ + phi(PqQ)] equal their values at τ.

`design_verdict9arbnet_ordS`: checked `ArbRowsOKSS d a T S T0` (ArbRowsOKS plus every reader's state window
counted from S ends by T0). Assumed: `hbase` (q constant on (S − phi PqQ, S]), `hQ` (QQuiet only for
q changes after S), `he : cleanS → ArbEdgeS` (ArbEdge with QClean only after S), ArbOrd, and the common
premises. The proof is `arbCond_of_ordS` + `cleanS_of_arb` (`qcleanFrom_of_quiet`, strong induction with a
last-change lemma; the base case uses hbase).

`b1_of_ord`: B1's clause from hord + hflip + hnore, with no band row. `ord_of_row`: the band row gives hord
for every warp in the bands. `ord_of_transport`: constant delays give hord iff `Dr + 2 ≤ DQ + Dq`.
`corner_ord`: delays within `(1 ± kn/kd)` of shared nominals with the κ-guarded nominal row are ordered. All
read as their names say.

### (c) My checks

1. **Composition.** I read the three verdict proofs (Events9FullArb.lean:207-310): each is one application of
   `design_verdict9arbnet` with `arbCond_of_ord` / `arbCond_of_ordS`. HARB7, HARB7' and HRst are not binders. The
   axiom audit is in the Lean section below.

2. **ArbOrd is a property of the chosen warp, not of the behaviour.** At a reader whose B1 band row fails
   (`lo Q + lo Dq < hi Dr + 2`), take any stretch after T0 on which the grant's request, its q and ustate's read
   of that request are constant for longer than the band widths. On that stretch the gate equations
   `y = s(wr) && e(wq)` and ustate's equation hold for any choice of the three warps inside their bands. With
   `wr = t − hi Dr`, `wq = t − lo Dq`, `fQ = t − lo Q`, the order fails at every such t. Splicing this choice into
   any witness keeps it monotone and inside the bands. For a late warp on a stretch [a, b], take `min(f t, a − hi)`
   before a, `t − hi` on [a, b] and `f` after b. For an early warp, take `f` before a, `t − lo` on [a, b] and
   `max(f t, b − lo)` after b. The changed reads all land where the source is constant, so the gate equations
   still hold. So there is a SatPin witness that violates ArbOrd. Since `tau hsat` is an unspecified `Classical.choose`,
   proving `ArbOrd hsat` for a concrete `x` means proving it for every witness, and that statement is false.
   Four-phase requests are idle for nanoseconds against band widths of 100-600 ps, so this applies to every
   realistic run, including the GLS runs where the point-delay order "holds". My Lean file formalises the core
   (idle stretch, one reader, the exact `b1_of_ord` hy/hord shapes):
   - `viol_order`: warps in the bands that satisfy the grant equation and violate the order at every t > T0, whenever `lo Q + lo Dq < hi Dr + 2`;
   - `sat_order`: other warps in the same bands satisfying the same equation and the order (if `lo Dr + 2 ≤ hi Q + hi Dq`);
   - `not_all_witnesses`: "every witness satisfies the order" is false at a B1-failing reader;
   - `merge6_instance`: the same with the committed seed-4 slow numbers of merge6.uarb1 g1 (Dr1 g1 (2898, 3477), Dq g1 (342, 450), printed B1 margin −225, so lo(P1Q) = 2912).

   Consequence: at the B1-failing readers (2-6 of 9 arbiters on the 8 slow/fast rows, 8-9 of 9 on gb 3/gb 4) the premise `ho` of the
   three verdicts cannot be supplied for any behaviour that idles. The "10 rows" therefore rest on a hypothesis
   that is undischargeable as stated, not merely unverified. The fix is to state the order inside the warp
   existential, i.e. SatPin strengthened with ArbOrd, or to condition it on the events b1_of_ord uses. The
   alternative is the AND-grant core proposed in f40e003a, which would drop ArbOrd altogether. ArbEdge and
   QQuiet also mention `tau hsat`, but they are event-guarded. When every input edge is far from every q flip
   (margins larger than the band widths), every witness satisfies them, so they can in principle be discharged
   from timing margins. ArbOrd's unguarded `∀ t > T0` cannot.

3. **Dropping ArbOrd breaks the B1 clause** (`b1_without_ord_false`). Request high on [2,19) ∪ [30,∞) read with
   delay 5, literal high on [0,21) read with delay 1, fQ = t − 1, T0 = 8. hy, both warps, Mono fQ, hT, hflip and
   hnore all hold, the B1 conclusion `y (t−2) = false` fails at t = 23, and hord fails there. This matches
   P14a's `w_rdok_b1_fails`. So ArbOrd (or the band row) is really needed by `b1_of_ord`; it is not decoration.

4. **Is ArbOrd behaviour that can be false on silicon?** In the fixed-delay reading (`ord_of_transport`) it is the
   order `Dr + 2 ≤ DQ + Dq` of three physical path delays. That order fails if the grant's request route is
   slower than ustate's request route plus q route. The research GLS at per-arc scatter 0.10 produced exactly
   that (−321 ps, §2) while every vector was computed correctly. So: (a) it can be false under plausible
   variation (κ unmeasured; the corner-model threshold is 0.047-0.067); (b) when it is false the theorem is
   silent about that run, and the run is not a counterexample to the circuit, only to the abstraction
   (consistent with `grant_fall_after_req_fall`, P14a §2); (c) the "covered" claim for a row is then vacuous for
   such behaviours. In the warp reading actually used in Lean, see check 2: it is not dischargeable at all.

5. **Joint satisfiability.** The repo has no witness for SatPin + constants + reset + ArbEdge(S) + QQuiet + ArbOrd
   + SelOK + SelPart on any gcd_hw route; 2ac6895b says so. By check 2, a witness at a B1-failing reader would
   need the existential reformulation first. A trivial witness (no request ever rises after reset) would satisfy
   QQuiet vacuously (no q change after S). It would not settle ArbOrd, which is about the chosen warps.
