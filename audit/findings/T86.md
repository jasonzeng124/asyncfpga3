# T86 -- select/latch monitor sweep at FAST_MIN over the board routes (own builds, own monitors T83, full coverage)

FAST_MIN is the corner where research's select race appeared. Each run: the bits-decoded netlist of the board route, SDF min
column, 83.3 MHz host, the audit's own vectors and oracle, monitors reporting every 5 us (the last report falls within 5 us of the
run's end). Steers: select settled before the request rises, no change while it is high. Muxes: select against z_req. Data
latches: closing-edge setup. `t83/sweep.txt`, `t83/logs/*_mon_fastmin.txt`.

**Reading.** On every board route checked, no steer or mux select changes while its request stands at FAST_MIN. The tightest
margins are at the xorshift exit mux umux3: 0.57 ns on item 14, 0.71 ns on item 4. Both are measured against umux3's `either`,
which leads z_req by one LUT, so they are slightly pessimistic. The select-unsafe uniform-scale items (4, 7) show no violation
here. Their margins (0.71, 1.51 ns at umux3) are smaller than those of the select-safe re-gates (9, 12: >= 3.3 ns), which
fits research's concern. Limits: one route per item, FAST_MIN only (item 14 also worst, T83), 1-bit control latches not
monitored, items 6 and 11 not in the sweep.
