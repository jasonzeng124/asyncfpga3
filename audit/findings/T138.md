# T138: research's model speed error (SIM-VS-BOARD b4f2ce555, BOARD-QUEUE e4d656078) -- own reproduction on August collatz

Claim: "the simulation at the ring band 0.288 runs 8-10% slower per iteration than the board".
- Research's collatz figures: FAST_MIN 65.16 ns/iter, SLOW_MAX 188.76, board 92.73, band 0.288 100.76 (+8.7 %).

## Own check
- Bitstream: the board's August collatz bitstream (research/board/aug/builds/collatz_bench_gen, bit md5 6f62bf4a50fa, as in research's README), decoded from the .bit by the full-stack harness, prjxray SDF of its route.
- Vectors: **own** (audit/t65/collatz_13.hex, steps up to 350), not the board's.
- Bench clock: 100 MHz, as on the board.
- Fit: own (audit/t138/fit.py), least-squares slope of the latency on the steps, over vectors >= 50 cycles.

| | own | research |
|---|---|---|
| oracle FAST_MIN / SLOW_MAX | 13/13, 13/13 | 29/29 at both corners |
| FAST_MIN ns/iter | 65.15 (9 vectors) | 65.16 |
| SLOW_MAX ns/iter | 188.78 (11 vectors) | 188.76 |
| board's 92.73 sits at x | 0.223 | 0.222 |
| band 0.288 vs board | +8.7 % | +8.7 % |

## Verdict
- **CONFIRMED on collatz.** Both corner slopes reproduce to 0.02 ns/iter with independent vectors, and the 8.7 % follows.
- Not reproduced (UNVERIFIED): collatz64 and ipow (+8.1 % each, research), and gcd (+9.9 %).
- xorshift has no August board comparison. Applying ~8 % to xorshift items (33 / 27 / 35) is an extrapolation.
