## Reading
* In own GLS every consumer of umux0's output captures at least 6.9 ns (item 31) / 10 ns (item 30) after the data settles.
* So neither research's rule M miss on item 30 (-1.2 ns vs z_req) nor the own late select at the data LUTs (101 / 100 ps after z_req, T126d/g) comes near a capture.
* What is violated is the request-edge criterion ("no select change while the output request is high"; M: data before z_req). Capture is not violated.
* This supports research's planned false-path argument: no consumer acts on the request edge.
* It rests on 14 / 13 own vectors. The close time is structural (a handshake phase), not data-dependent.
* item 31's ulink_n11 is not covered by this run.
* Item 29's umux2 M row is not checked here.
