## Summary
All four claims reproduce from clean. BQ#3's full stack is 16/16 with errs=0 at FAST_MIN, SLOW_MAX and band. The lap counts are identical to research's, and the sdf/xray-sdf md5s match the records. My own judge also passes: it checks the delivered values against dynamatic's gcd.c, and it fails on a corrupted oracle. The lut-pinmap negative control reproduces exactly: 9/16, dead set 5,6,7,8,11,12,13, errs=0; patched 16/16. The one-corner bd_prims_sim.v numbers are prjxray's, and the three benches pass at both corners. They fail under logic mutations, and under a timing mutation they fail exactly at the documented margin. xray_delay's routing-BEL charges equal prjxray-db's to ±1 ps on 16,404 OUTMUX sinks and 192 FFMUX sinks. seeds_fid.py reports INCOMPLETE for all 6 forms of a missing verdict. Minor open items:
1. tb_ctl's SDELAY=1 comment is stale, and the bench has no timing teeth on the simple pipe's internal request delay.
2. seeds_fid.py reads stale sweep logs if a work dir is reused.
3. xray_delay uses the db's `a5lut` arc rather than the `o5` arc for O5->xMUX, without saying why (it is the larger one).
4. The negative control is an emulated defect at SLOW_MAX only, as research already discloses.
