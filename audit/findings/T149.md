# T149: research's compiler suite (4fc96e310, 5a8053b31, 38c58b2c4 kernels/SUITE.md) -- expected values and honesty

## Expected values: CONFIRMED by an own oracle
- Tool: audit/t149/own_oracle.py.
- Method:
  - each kernel's own C is compiled with an own driver (Dynamatic's Integration.h stubbed, in_int_t taken from the kernel's own header);
  - the two bench words are mapped to the parameters from the signature (an int64 parameter = hi:lo);
  - every vector in kernels/K/vec.hex is run and the result compared with research's vec.exp.
- 14 scalar kernels with vectors match on every vector, 0 differing: straight, countloop, sqrtsub, nest3, twoloops, brhash, lfsrfind, quadrec, horner, fold64 (i64), varshift, cmpchain, nested, branchloop.

## The bench-mask lesson (T141) is built in
- research/tools/suite/oracle.sh reads cells/hw/gen_bench.py DOMAIN_RESTRICTIONS. It refuses (exit 2) any kernel the bench masks and points to kernel_oracle.sh, which applies the masks.
- The masked kernels (collatz, collatz64, xorshift) use kernel_oracle.sh in the table.

## The table states its limits
- Failures stay in, marked:
  - deadlocks: nest3 default, quadrec both flows, nested default;
  - digitsum stops in the frontend;
  - digitdiv is refused by bdc;
  - memsum waits for the bench ABI.
- "One seed is a smoke test, not a result", and no ns/iteration is quoted.
- Not chosen to flatter the flow: three of the 14 new shapes deadlock or are refused in at least one flow.

## Not checked
- The simcheck and routed PASS columns (pre-route sim, one seed at worst). They are smoke tests by research's own description.
