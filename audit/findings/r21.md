## Task 2 -- derivation and corner

Derivation (arbtie.md):
- R0 rises with the 2nd request edge, so t_R0 = t_qend + skew.
- q commits at most t_qend + trp_max.
- g_k falls at ua_k at t_commit + GP + c.
- A0 acts at ua_k at t_R0 + RT + a.
- Safe iff RT + a + skew >= trp_max + GP + c. This is algebraically right.

Corner handling is wrong:
- Early side: RT and a, plus R0's branch of skew (r -> LUT5 arc). These need their MIN.
- Late side: GP and c, plus q's branch of skew (r -> LUT6 arc). These need their MAX.
- FAST_MIN everywhere is a valid lower bound only for the early side.
- The docstring says "mixing columns is not a PVT point". That is true for global corners, but a race check needs at least an OCV derate.
- The SLOW_MAX "margins grow" argument rests on the model's fixed trp_max of 647. A real tau scales with the slow corner.

Numbers on BQ#3 seed d are in own_margin.txt. In mixed-corner skew the shared request wire cancels, because the r->LUT6 and r->LUT5 SDF wires are equal for all 18. The table's skmx column does not cancel it. The break-even derate line does.

Missed term: R0 rising off q's own runt. It does not bind on a LUT6_2, because the LUT6 and LUT5 share the pin.
