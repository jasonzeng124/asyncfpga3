### 1.4 Residual vacuity for small T
- Toy: `toy_no_model`: for T < D, NO behaviour satisfies Behaves ∧ ArbOK T ∧ Env, so NetCorrect and
  NetCorrectSeq hold vacuously. Mechanism: q must flip D ps after A0's fall (its inputs are then constant), and
  ArbOK T with T < D forbids any q move later than T after the last input edge.
- In general: every q flip of a GateOK behaviour is caused by a change of a pin read, i.e. comes at least the
  pin's admissible delay after an edge on that pin (or on the feedback pin, which itself follows an earlier q
  flip). For T below the smallest admissible delay of the state LUT's ins arcs, ArbOK T therefore forbids
  every q flip after time T that does not happen to have another ins edge within T before it. On the target
  (my numbers from the decoded net, ins pins excluding the VCC pin): fmin from 78 to 136 ps, smin 192 to 344
  ps, smax 640 to 664 ps; so the threshold is between 78/g ps (fast corner) and 664·g ps (slow corner).
- Stated? B's ArbOKRepair (dc3db336) states it ("`arbOK_needs_T_ge_arc` ... THE theorem must take T at least the
  state gate's slowest admissible arc, or it is vacuous on every arbitration. (Recorded for shard A: STATUS/Spec
  should say so.)"). The Spec does not: §7 says "for stated p = ⟨g, κ⟩, T, R, su, Δo" for NetCorrectSeq and
  "¬ NetCorrect ... (for every T, at g = 1)" for the refutation.
- Harmful? For the refutation: no (a refutation at T covers every T' ≥ T by `netCorrect_anti`; A's plan uses
  T = 656 ps, one above the 655 ps slow end it assumed). But §7's "for every T" is unattainable: for T below the
  arc, runs in which an arbiter switches are excluded, so ¬NetCorrect cannot be shown with them (the toy is a
  proof that at T < D the statement can be vacuously true). For the positive NetCorrectSeq: harmful if the
  stated T is below the slowest state-LUT arc·g: then any proof says nothing about runs with arbitrations. The
  owner should require the stated T ≥ max over the 9 arbiters of the ins arcs' smax·g (664·g ps), and read
  "the theorem's value is the largest T" as for g, κ (the Spec says this for g, κ, not for T).
Verdict: residual vacuity CONFIRMED; stated by B, not in the Spec; harmless only if the final theorem's T is
checked against the arc bound (nothing in the statement enforces it).
