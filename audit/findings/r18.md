## Method
- Bench: `audit/r18/tb_arbtie.v`. It is my own, written independently of formal's `Wip/GLSResolve/sim/tb_arb.v`, though structurally close because both follow gen.py. Research sources are read in place from ~/wt-r17 and are not copied.
- Each trial: a lone transaction puts q at QP. After idle, the client that must flip q rises at t0 and the other at t0+W. W is swept in 1 ps steps (A: 200..460, B: -50..250). Clients are four-phase, and the server is A0 <= #S R0 (transport).
- A violation is a trial with server A0 rises < A1 rises + A2 rises.
- Seeds: +META_SEED 0..19 (S in 200, 600, 1500, 3000, 7000) and 0..9 (S in 700..1300). Controls: +META_X=1 and +META_OFF=1, 5 seeds each at A S=600.
- Separations: in A, violations fall at W=223..438 (pin-time separation is about +15 ps). The spread comes from the N(0, WIN/4) noise in delta, centred near TLOOP − WIN/2. In B they fall at W=10..212.
- Reproduce: `source ~/audit-tc/openxc7/export.sh; audit/r18/run.sh` (2 sims in parallel, about 1.5 min). The aggregate is in `audit/r18/summary.txt`, per-trial lines in `audit/r18/logs/`.
