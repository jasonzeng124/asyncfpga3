# T83 -- latch setup/hold margins on board item 14's route (research's open question: uaddi0's i+1 "worst settle 3.8 ns exceeds its on-cycle delay 2.85 ns", "min margin -0.79 ns at some load")

Own monitor `t83/mon_latch.py` (plugged in through build_ps.sh's PS_MON_GEN hook): for every 32-bit bd_latch of the kernel
(22 latches, 704 LUTs; the 1-bit control latches are not mapped), at the LUT's own input pins after the per-sink interconnect
delay, the enable's closing edge (1 -> 0) against the last change of each data pin (setup), and the first data change within 2 ns
after closing (hold). Enable and data are told apart by net: the enable is the one net every LUT of a latch shares, data pins
carry the data_in nets, feedback pins (the latch's own outputs) and constants are excluded.

## Results, item 14 board route (pmf_1, sdf 169ce4d6), own 17 vectors, 83.3 MHz (`t83/logs/`)

**Latches (closing-edge setup / hold), worst case.** All 22 data latches: min setup **9.9 ns** (ulink_n_trueResult_10 stage 1) to
222 ns; ulink_n30__1 (the i+1 latch) **15.5 ns**. Min hold (first data change after closing) 1.18 ns (ulink_n8) and 1.46 ns
(ulink_n7); none closer. These latches open when the request arrives and close at its return to zero, so closing-edge setup is
large by construction. It is not where i+1 timing is exposed.

**Steers (request-edge sampling: req0 = req.~s, req1 = req.s).** All 16 steer LUTs (8 cond_br pairs). The loop condition
n31 (ucmpi1's compare of i+1) feeds ucond_br6-9.

| corner | min rise margin (s settled before req rises) | s changed while req high |
|---|---|---|
| worst (chains 0.21, CARRY4/F7F8/FF 1, rest 0.288, + M scatter) | **1.67 ns** (ucond_br6), loop steers 1.67-1.92 ns over 2077 rises each | **0** |
| FAST_MIN | **1.77 ns** (ucond_br6) | **0** |

Coverage: the monitor reported every 50 us in this first run. These figures cover 100 of 116.7 us (worst) and 50 of 94.9 us (FAST_MIN). The run's
results are 17/17 against the own oracle.

**Reading.** Research's open question (uaddi0's i+1 "worst settle 3.8 ns exceeds its on-cycle delay 2.85 ns", "min margin -0.79 ns at
some load", JOURNAL 2026-11-12 18:00 / 20:40) is a rule-level margin with research's guard band. On this route, at the worst and
FAST_MIN corners, the place where the i+1 compare is actually sampled (the loop steers) has **+1.67 ns of raw margin** and no
glitch while the request is high. Not covered: the 1-bit control latches, bd_mux selects, other routes, and the SLOW_MAX column
(the slow-band chains scale with the logic there). **Verdict: the open question does not show up as a violation on item 14's board
route. The margin is positive and modest.**
