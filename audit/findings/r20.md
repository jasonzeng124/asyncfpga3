## New / remaining failure modes of the new resolver (adversarial, tb_adv.v)

1. **A drive toward a pending decision voids it -> non-monotone latching.** A runt that went metastable with
   choose = runt value (commit to 1 pending, trp up to 482 ps) is BROKEN by a second drive to 1 (BREAK voids the
   commit, kc_next++), and if that second drive is itself a runt the node takes a fresh coin flip. nonmono.log:
   C-element, b = one 100 ps pulse vs two 100 ps pulses 10 ps apart: 3/40 copies that latch with one pulse do
   NOT latch with two (H=150: 5/40). Example new/adv_np2_h100.log copy 5: `META t=7150 ... -> 1 after 205 ps`,
   `BREAK t=7160 drive to 1`, `META t=7260 ... -> 0` -> final 0. The old resolver ignored the second pulse
   entirely (NP1 = NP2 per copy). A physical node already resolving toward 1 and driven to 1 again would not fall
   back; the model's "the drive wins" rule makes more drive able to reduce latching.
2. **No integration across a pulse train.** Each runt is judged by p = time since the last accepted edge only.
   b high 20 ps / low 1 ps x 50 (1.05 ns, 95 % duty, 5 TLOOP) never latches the C-element: 0/40, and q emits
   up to 100 edges to fanout (new); old also 0/40 but 10 edges. 10 x 100 ps pulses latch only 14-15/40
   (train.log). Not a regression (old equally lost), but "nothing is ignored" does not mean such input is
   honoured.
3. **Wrong feedback mask is silent.** FB6 naming a non-feedback pin (A1) or an extra pin (A2|A3) gives a dead
   C-element (q=0 for a 300 ps b pulse, 0 edges, 40/40) with no warning (mask.log). Only an all-zero mask is
   checked. In the GLS flow the mask comes from gen.py's SDF self-loop scan (gen.py:695-702), so this bites hand
   benches only; nothing cross-checks that the masked pin is actually driven by the output.
4. **Unrecognised state nodes** (loops through >1 cell; gen.py:684 "NOT detected (stated limitation)") get no
   resolver at all = pure transport, where a runt circulates forever (log_W20_off.txt). Unchanged by 055edd74.
5. **Multi-pin feedback**: TLOOP = min over the feedback pins (gen.py:697) and fb reaches all masked pins at
   that one time, so a slower second feedback pin is early in the model. By reading, not simulated.
6. **x pulse shorter than TLOOP is absorbed** (xin.log BX=30/100: q ends 0 in 40/40, transport gives x/oscillation):
   both old and new; X-optimistic relative to the transport ablation.
